How it works

FujiSync communicates with your camera over PTP (Picture Transfer Protocol) via USB-C OTG — the same mode your camera uses for its own backup and restore function (USB RAW CONV. / BACKUP RESTORE). No Fujifilm SDK or licensed library is used, and no attempt is made to present the app as licensed software.
